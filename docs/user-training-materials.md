## Islamic Features Training

### Prayer Times System

#### Understanding Prayer Time Accuracy
```markdown
**Our Commitment to Accuracy:**

✅ **Primary Source**: Aladhan API (internationally recognized)
✅ **Secondary Backup**: IslamicFinder calculations  
✅ **Local Verification**: Astronomical calculations for your location
✅ **Islamic Authority Oversight**: Local Islamic scholars verify times
✅ **Manual Override Available**: Emergency adjustments when needed

**What You See:**
- **Fajr** (Dawn Prayer): Starts before sunrise
- **Sunrise**: When sun becomes visible (no prayer, just reference)  
- **Dhuhr** (Midday Prayer): After sun passes zenith
- **Asr** (Afternoon Prayer): Mid-afternoon timing
- **Maghrib** (Sunset Prayer): Immediately after sunset
- **Isha** (Night Prayer): After twilight disappears

**Color Coding:**
- 🟢 Green: Current prayer time
- 🟡 Yellow: Next prayer approaching (within 30 minutes)  
- ⚪ White: Completed prayer times
- 🔴 Red: Prayer time missed (with gentle reminder)
```

#### Using the Qibla Compass
```markdown
**Finding the Direction to Mecca:**

**On Web Platform:**
1. Go to Prayer Times page
2. Click "Qibla Direction" button
3. Allow location access when prompted
4. Red arrow points toward Mecca
5. Rotate until arrow aligns with your prayer mat

**On Mobile App:**
1. Open app and tap compass icon
2. Hold phone flat (like a table)
3. Allow location and compass access
4. Slowly rotate until arrow points to Mecca
5. Green indicator shows when properly aligned

**Troubleshooting Qibla Direction:**
- Ensure location services are enabled
- Move away from metal objects or electronics
- Recalibrate phone compass (wave in figure-8 pattern)
- In buildings, go near a window for better GPS signal
```

### Islamic Calendar Integration

#### Understanding Hijri Dates
```markdown
**Why Islamic Dates Matter:**

The Islamic calendar follows lunar months, making it essential for:
- **Ramadan**: Fasting month timing  
- **Hajj**: Pilgrimage season
- **Islamic Holidays**: Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha
- **Holy Days**: Day of Arafah, Laylat al-Qadr
- **Educational Content**: Seasonal Islamic teachings

**How We Calculate Hijri Dates:**
1. **Astronomical Calculation**: Based on moon phases
2. **Local Verification**: Confirmed with Islamic authorities
3. **Global Awareness**: Different regions may vary by 1 day
4. **Community Consensus**: Following local mosque decisions

**Displayed Information:**
- Today's Hijri date prominently shown
- Upcoming Islamic events highlighted  
- Educational content aligned with Islamic seasons
- Community events scheduled using Islamic calendar
```

```markdown
**Islamic Educational Philosophy:**

Islam emphasizes learning "from cradle to grave," but content must be appropriate for spiritual and intellectual development stages.

**Children (Ages 3-12):**
- **Focus**: Love of Allah, Prophet Muhammad (PBUH), basic prayers
- **Content Types**: Stories, simple duas, moral lessons
- **Examples**: Prophet stories, learning Fatiha, kindness to parents
- **Assessment**: Fun quizzes, completion badges

**Youth (Ages 13-18):**

- **Content Types**: Character building, Islamic history, social issues
- **Examples**: Companions' stories, Islamic ethics, community responsibility  
- **Assessment**: Reflective questions, discussion participation

**Adults (Ages 18-65):**
- **Focus**: Comprehensive Islamic knowledge, leadership, application
- **Content Types**: Detailed fiqh, Quran commentary, contemporary issues
- **Examples**: Marriage in Islam, business ethics, parenting guidance
```

## Troubleshooting & Support

### Common Issues and Solutions

#### Prayer Times Issues
```markdown
**Problem: Prayer times seem incorrect for my location**

**Solution Steps:**
1. Check your location settings (allow GPS access)
2. Verify your timezone is correct in profile settings
3. Compare with local mosque or Islamic center
4. Contact Islamic authority if discrepancy continues
5. Use manual override if immediate correction needed

**Problem: Qibla direction doesn't seem right**

**Solution Steps:**
1. Ensure location services are enabled
2. Move away from large metal objects
3. Recalibrate compass (wave phone in figure-8 pattern)
4. Compare with known Qibla direction (mosque orientation)
5. Use backup calculation methods in settings
```

#### Educational System Issues
```markdown
**Problem: Can't access age-appropriate content**

**Solution Steps:**
1. Verify your age tier setting in profile
2. Clear browser cache (web) or restart app (mobile)
3. Check if content is temporarily unavailable
4. Contact support with specific content title

**Problem: Progress not syncing between devices**

**Solution Steps:**
1. Ensure you're logged into the same account
2. Check internet connection on both devices
3. Force sync in settings menu
4. Log out and log back in to refresh connection
5. Contact technical support if issue persists
```

#### Community Features Issues
```markdown
**Problem: Not receiving community notifications**

**Solution Steps:**
1. Check notification preferences in profile settings
2. Verify email address is correct and accessible
3. Check spam/junk folder for community emails
4. Ensure push notifications are enabled (mobile)
5. Contact community administrator for notification testing
```
