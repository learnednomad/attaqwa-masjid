# 📚 SEERAH CURRICULUM - ACADEMIC RESOURCES
## Based on Authentic Sources: The Sealed Nectar, Ibn Kathir, and Imam al-Baghawi

### بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم

This comprehensive Seerah curriculum is developed with strict adherence to Islamic jurisprudence and authentic sources.

## 📖 Primary Sources
1. **Ar-Raheeq Al-Makhtum** (The Sealed Nectar) - Sheikh Safi-ur-Rahman al-Mubarakpuri
2. **Al-Bidayah wan-Nihayah** - Imam Ibn Kathir
3. **Sharh as-Sunnah & Ma'alim at-Tanzil** - Imam al-Baghawi

## ⚖️ Jurisprudential Standards
- ✅ Only authenticated (sahih) narrations used
- ✅ Isnad (chain of narration) referenced for controversial topics
- ✅ Strict adherence to Ahlus Sunnah wal Jamaah methodology
- ✅ Age-appropriate content filtering applied
- ❌ No Israeliyyat without proper verification
- ❌ No exaggerated miracle stories
- ❌ No sectarian interpretations

## 🎯 Educational Objectives
1. Develop deep love and understanding of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ
2. Strengthen Islamic identity through authentic knowledge
3. Extract practical lessons for contemporary Muslim life
4. Build critical thinking in hadith authentication
5. Foster connection to classical Islamic scholarship

## 📊 Implementation Guide
- **Elementary**: Focus on character and basic stories
- **Middle School**: Historical context and moral lessons
- **High School**: Source criticism and jurisprudential derivations
- **Adults**: Complete scholarly approach with contemporary application
